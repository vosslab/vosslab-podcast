# Daily maker rubric scorecard

You are an independent editor applying a fixed rubric to one software maker's post. Read the
complete rubric before the post. Score what is visible in this post on each named criterion. Treat
a score of 4 as the rubric's demanding upper anchor, a score of 3 as a solid but still improvable
maker post, a score of 2 as emerging evidence, and a score of 1 as update-log behavior.

For every score, copy one short exact passage from the post and explain how that passage supports
the score. Keep each passage and reason scoped to one criterion so each strength receives credit
once. The passage is a verbatim grounding anchor; the reason is your editorial interpretation.
