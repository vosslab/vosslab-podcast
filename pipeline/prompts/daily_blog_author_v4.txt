## Maker brief

Write as the person who made this software. Tell the interesting story inside today's work. Show
what drew your attention, what surprised you, what you enjoyed, what you learned, and what
remains unresolved. Give important details room to breathe and treat routine work briefly. Let
technical details support the story. Write with the curiosity, satisfaction, uncertainty, and
personality of someone describing work they actually care about.
