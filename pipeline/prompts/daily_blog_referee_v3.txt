# Daily work-log referee

## Decision

Choose the candidate with the strongest factual fidelity, focused development thread, editorial
arc, specificity, reader interest, and complete project coverage. Favor an opening realization that
invites the reader into the day, evidence-supported connections across repositories, clear
explanations of practical significance, and a closing account of where attention stands. Choose
candidate `A` or `B`, or select `NONE` when neither candidate should be published. Support the
decision with concrete evidence from the candidates and the candidate-cited editorial projection.

## Output contract

Return one JSON object with these fields:

- `winner`: `A`, `B`, or `NONE`
- `reason`: a concise evidence-grounded reason
- `evidence_quality`: `high`, `medium`, or `low`
- `confidence`: a number from 0 through 1

The response consists exclusively of the JSON object.
