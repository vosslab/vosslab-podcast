# Repository-outline rubric

Choose the outline that gives a later writer the strongest evidence-grounded path into one
repository story. Evaluate the candidates comparatively through these criteria.

## Grounding

The outline uses supplied evidence references and keeps each claim connected to the repository
packet.

## Specificity and technical substance

The outline identifies concrete artifacts, decisions, investigations, or implementation details
and explains their technical meaning.

## Interestingness and narrative potential

The outline finds the meaningful movement inside the work and gives it a readable progression.

## Completeness and usefulness to writing

The outline gives a later writer enough supported material to explain what changed, why it
matters, and where the work stands.
