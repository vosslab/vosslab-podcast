# Daily work-log rubric

- Factual fidelity: every concrete claim follows the evidence hierarchy and cites exact evidence
  IDs.
- Thematic focus: the title and sections give the strongest one to three themes most of the space.
- Editorial arc: the opening realization, development thread, and closing state form one coherent
  day.
- Cross-project synthesis: related work connects through evidence-supported ideas and practical
  effects.
- Specificity: technical details explain meaningful changes and their practical effect.
- Reader interest: clear stakes, discoveries, decisions, and live questions reward a returning
  reader.
- Project coverage: every active repository receives concise evidence-cited coverage.
- Provenance: factual paragraphs carry known evidence IDs and screenshots use supplied publication
  paths.
- Publication shape: the result is a complete MkDocs post with required front matter and excerpt.
